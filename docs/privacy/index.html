Privacy

Your data stays on your Mac. SwiftDB connects only to the destinations you choose.

Last updated

September 10, 2026

What SwiftDB does not do

SwiftDB is a local macOS database client. It has no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or third-party crash reporting. It does not collect, sell, or share database contents or personal data.

Stored on your Mac

Connection profiles, query history, saved queries, and preferences stay on your Mac. Passwords and SSH key passphrases are stored in macOS Keychain. Security-scoped bookmarks let SwiftDB reopen files you explicitly select.

Network access

SwiftDB talks to the database and SSH destinations you configure. The Direct edition can inspect a local Docker-compatible daemon when you ask it to discover databases. Data those systems return is processed on your Mac. Checking for an app update contacts the SwiftDB download site.

Questions

Send privacy requests through GitHub Issues.
